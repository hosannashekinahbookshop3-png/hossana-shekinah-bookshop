HOSSANA SHEKINAH BOOKSHOP — CLOUDFLARE PAGES PACKAGE

DEPLOY
1. Extract this ZIP on your phone/computer.
2. In Cloudflare Pages, use the direct upload / drag-and-drop project workflow.
3. Upload the extracted folder contents (index.html, styles.css, app.js, products.json, robots.txt, sitemap.xml). If Cloudflare asks for a folder, select this folder after extracting.
4. Deploy.

IMPORTANT
- This is a static storefront with a browser-side cart and WhatsApp order handoff. It does not store orders or process payments.
- Bank transfer details are displayed in the checkout. Customers should wait for your WhatsApp confirmation before transferring.
- Delivery fee is not calculated automatically; confirm it based on delivery location.
- Product prices are set to half the reference list price captured during preparation. Supplier prices, availability and your actual stock can change; verify before fulfilling orders.
- Product titles and source list prices were gathered from Rovingheights Books and Scripture Haven Co. The site uses original text-only decorative covers, not copied supplier product photography or descriptions.
- True customer-submitted persistent product reviews are not included because they require a database or review service. You can add a Google Reviews link or backend later.
- The social media account URLs and logo were not available in the materials accessible for this build, so they have not been fabricated. Add official profile links and your logo when available.
- The canonical URL and sitemap use hossanashekinahbookshop.pages.dev; update them if Cloudflare assigns a different hostname.
